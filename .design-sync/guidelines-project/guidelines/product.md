# Product Intent

## The user and their moment

A single powerlifting athlete, training under a coach who programs work in a Google Sheet. The athlete opens the app mid-Session, on the gym floor, between Sets: glancing down to see what is prescribed, logging what they actually did, and moving on. Context is physical and time-sliced — chalky hands, attention on the bar, competing with rest timers and the next Set. The coach never touches the app.

## Purpose

Surface the coach-programmed training and let the athlete log Sets without friction. The Sheet is authoritative; the app makes "what am I doing today?" and "log this Set" instant. Success is the athlete logging every Set in the moment, trusting it round-trips to the coach's Sheet correctly, and never thinking about the spreadsheet underneath.

## Personality

A supportive training partner, not a hype machine. **Warm, focused, trustworthy.** The emotional goal is quiet confidence: accompanied and on-track, never nagged, gamified, or shouted at. Celebration is earned and occasional — tied to genuine milestones like Move On, not sprinkled on every tap.

## Anti-references

- **Bro-y hype / aggro fitness.** No neon-on-black "beast mode", flames, all-caps shouting, or streaks-as-pressure.
- **Spreadsheet-in-an-app.** It is a Sheet client but must never look like one: no dense grids, tiny tap targets, raw cell editing, or visible row/column plumbing.
- **Generic fitness SaaS.** No interchangeable rounded-card grids, stock gradients, ring charts everywhere, badge spam, or cheerful clip art.
- **Over-gamified.** No XP bars, confetti on every tap, mascots, points, levels, or streak pressure.

## Design principles

- **Log in the moment, think later.** Every screen optimizes for the athlete mid-Set: the next action is obvious and reachable, prescription and logging sit together, and nothing demands attention the bar should have.
- **Hide the spreadsheet.** The athlete sees Sessions, Exercises, Sets, and Set Logs — never plumbing.
- **Earned celebration.** Acknowledgement scales with the achievement.
- **Trust the round-trip.** Surface sync state and pending writes honestly; never imply a save that has not landed.
- **Sync automatically, recover manually.** Manual sync is a recovery action in Settings ("Sync now"), never a primary Session control.
- **Respect the athlete's expertise.** Show real numbers, loads, RPE, and history plainly, and trust the athlete to read them.
