# Workout App glossary: Dictation

Part of the domain glossary in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md), which indexes every term. These
terms lean on the Logging terms in [logging.md](logging.md).

### Dictation

**Spoken Set**: A Set as the athlete said it, read from a speech transcript: some of a Set Log's weight, reps, and RPE ("185 for 5 at 8", "RPE 9", "bodyweight for 12"), "same again", or "skip". Each value it names replaces the one the Set's card would pre-fill, and the rest keep the pre-fill. A value whose word was said last with its number cut off, as the RPE of "185 for 5 at" or "at 8 and a" or the reps of "185 for", is missing, never taken from the pre-fill, unless its engine's alternative transcripts that hear the Set's other values the same all hear it the same: then they settle it. Where the top transcript wrote a number in the cut-off value's place, as the 9 after the misheard "at" of "135 for 8 and 9", the alternatives settle it only as that number, or as that number with the half or decimal the top cut off after it, as the 9.5 of "at 9 and a"; where it wrote none, as after "135 for 8 at", they supply it, because they are the same engine hearing the same audio. Where an alternative is the top transcript with words before it, as "10 for 12 at 8" beside "for 12 at 8", the recognizer cut off the start of what was said, and those alternatives read the Set when every one that reads agrees. Where an alternative's first number is longer than the top's and ends in it, as "225 for 3" beside "25 for 3", the top may have lost the start of that number, so it writes nothing. Where the top has a number no word places and an alternative is the top with only "at" added, as "275 for 4 at 9" beside "275 for 4 9", the recognizer lost the "at", and those alternatives read the Set when every one that reads agrees. A number no word labels is the weight when it leads and words label both the reps and the RPE after it, as the 135 of "135, 8 reps, RPE 9", unless it ends in 4, because "eighty for" sounds as "eighty-four" and the recognizer writes "80 for 5 reps at 8" as "84 5 reps at 8". A number of 1,000 or more, in digits ("1,005") or words ("one thousand five", "eleven hundred", "ten fifty"), is the weight only where it cannot hide another value: "for" right after it and words labelling the reps and the RPE ("1005 for 1 at 10"), or "pounds" right after it and words labelling the reps ("1005 pounds for 1"), because "1358" may be "135 for 8" and the 4 of "1854 5 reps" the "for" of "185 for 5". With all three present the Set is Logged through the same write as a Set Log entered on the card; "same again" logs the structured Set Log of the nearest earlier Set of the same Exercise in the Session, the walk a Load Basis takes, and "skip" Skips the Set. When a value is still missing, nothing is written and the Set stays Pending, and the outcome names the missing values. Words outside its grammar, a number no word places, kilograms, numbers the recognizer ran together, a transcript that reads as two Sets, values no Set Log holds, values no lifter logs (more than 100 reps, or a weight finer than a quarter pound), and an RPE off the ten points reject the transcript rather than guess at it. Avoid: voice command, utterance.

**Dictation**: Capturing a Spoken Set by speech: holding the Active Set Card's mic, or hands-free from the Action button, Siri, Shortcuts, a Log a Set control, or the rest Live Activity's mic, which listen on the Current Session's active Set until the athlete stops talking. The card's mic also shows on a logged Set reopened for review: what is said there saves over its Set Log as the review's own save does, filling what is left unsaid from what the review card shows, with no rest and no move on, and a "skip" said there writes nothing. With the app on screen the card listens and holds the written Set; the hold ends when the app leaves the screen. With the app in the background, the phone locked or not, the Dictation runs headless: it listens, fills what is left unsaid from what the card shows for the Set as a release of the card's mic would, writes through the same store calls, moves the card on with the write, starts the rest the stage would (one rest per process, which the rest pill, the Live Activity, and the rest-end notification all read), reads back on the Live Activity (Listening, Logged, Skipped, or Not logged with why), and flushes, with nothing spoken back and the app never coming forward. A headless press that finds the card holding a Set its mic wrote ends that hold, as a touch of the card would, and listens for the next Set. A headless press that finds no Pending Set in the Current Session, or no Current Session, listens for nothing and says so on the Live Activity. With Live Activities off for the app nothing can show the recording, so a headless press listens for nothing and the surface that pressed shows "Turn on Live Activities for WorkoutTracker to log while locked". It is the speech-to-text step that hears the athlete and hands the Spoken Set its transcripts, not the Set itself. Those transcripts are a Live Transcript and, when a second engine read the press's recording, a Recording Transcript. Avoid: voice logging, background recording.

**Live Transcript**: The transcripts of a Dictation from the engine that listened while the athlete spoke, the most likely first, as it hands them over when the press ends. Its alternatives are the same engine hearing the same audio, so they may settle a value its most likely transcript cut off, or restore the start or an "at" it lost. While the athlete speaks, the same engine captions the card, and hands-free listening stops 1.2 seconds after the caption last changed if a Pause has not stopped it first. That caption lags the voice by seconds, because the engine releases it in blocks of about 3.9 seconds of audio. Avoid: partial transcript, streaming transcript, SpeechAnalyzer transcript.

**Recording Transcript**: The one transcript a second engine reads from a press's recording, with no alternatives. It reads the whole recording once the press ends, or, listening hands-free, the recording up to a Pause when the athlete said nothing after it. A press that has one is decided by it, and the Live Transcript decides a press that has none (ADR-0021). The Live Transcript's alternatives never settle a value the Recording Transcript cut off, because they come from another engine. Avoid: Whisper transcript, final transcript, batch transcript.

**Pause**: The quiet on a hands-free press's mic after the athlete spoke, read from the level of each 85 ms of its recording. The noise floor is the 15th percentile of the levels so far, a frame 9 dB over it is loud, two loud frames in a row are speech, and the quiet frames since the last loud one are the Pause, so one loud frame starts it over. At 341 ms the second engine reads the recording so far. A Recording Transcript that names the whole Set by itself, every value, "skip", or "same again", and writes stops listening there, or at 1024 ms when its RPE is a whole number under 10, because "and a half" may follow. A Pause of 1.2 seconds stops listening whatever was read (ADR-0021). Avoid: gap, endpoint.

**Speech Model**: The model the second engine reads a press's recording with, Whisper small at a pinned revision with prompt p1. It is downloaded, never bundled, and only on a network that is neither expensive nor in Low Data Mode. Core ML prepares it for the phone's chip once after each download and each OS update. The first press that finds it out of memory starts loading it, and it leaves memory on a memory warning, after 30 minutes with no press, or when the phone runs critically hot. A press reads with it only once it is ready, so a press that finds it anywhere else has no Recording Transcript, and the Live Transcript decides. Settings > Speech shows where it stands, and each Dictation Record names where it stood when the press began and ended. Avoid: Whisper model, ASR model, ML model.

**Decision Deadline**: How long after a press ends its outcome may wait for the Recording Transcript, the live engine's finalize included: 1.5 seconds. A Recording Transcript already in decides the press whatever the clock says; one still being read at the deadline is dropped, its read cancelled, and the Live Transcript decides. It is a safety bound, not the target: a press should decide within 0.5 seconds, and each press is measured against that (ADR-0021). While a press waits, the card keeps its caption with the mic off. Avoid: Whisper timeout, decode timeout, grace period.

**Dictation Record**: What one press of a Dictation leaves behind, one per press, whether it decided or was cancelled: which Set it was for and what its card showed, the Live Transcript, the Recording Transcript or why there was none, the Speech Model version and prompt it would be read with, the outcome and what each rule would have decided from the same transcripts, when each phase happened, how long the press waited against the 0.5 second target and the largest share of a miss, the longest the main thread took to answer during it, and the phone's state. It holds no audio. A press hands its record to a journal on the phone and moves on without waiting for the write. Settings > Speech exports the journal through the share sheet, with the MetricKit payloads iOS delivered and a label for each record whose outcome wrote a Set. The label is that Set's Set State and Set Log at export, so the same Set Log says the write stood, another that the athlete corrected it, and Pending that they cleared it. A record whose Set is gone has no label. `workout dictations summary` and `workout dictations replay` read an export, and score each rule's outcomes against its labels. Avoid: recording, dictation log, press log, telemetry event.
