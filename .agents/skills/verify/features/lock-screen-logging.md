# Lock Screen Logging

<!-- doc-symbols: not-in-tree ListCell ReduceMotionEnabled -->

During a rest the Lock Screen card is the rest timer for the Set the stage's card is on. Its rest face shows the countdown, the Cadence under it, and the weight the stage holds with the coach's Reps and RPE, each under its unit. A Set is logged in three taps without unlocking: Log, the Reps, then the RPE. The weight cannot change here. The last tap runs in the app's process, through the same store call as the Log capsule, so the rest restarts, the card moves to the next Set, and the write queues. A lighter green fills the card when the rest starts, and the fill's right edge runs left on the system clock until the rest is over, with no update from the app.

## Sub-features

- `lock-screen-rest` draws the countdown, the bare Cadence, and `LBS`, `REPS`, `RPE` over their values, with Log in the bottom corner and the mic and the note button in the top corner. A Set with no Cadence moves the numbers up under the countdown. A value the Set has none of draws a muted dash.
- `lock-screen-note` opens the Coach Note where the numbers were, on two lines at most, and a longer note ends in `…`. Log becomes the close button, the mic and the note button leave, and the countdown and the Cadence hold still. An Exercise with no Coach Note has no note button.
- `lock-screen-reps` turns the card to `Reps` with the weight beside it and five Reps Chips (`3` to `7`), a tick under each prescribed Reps. A Reps tap turns the card to `RPE` with `252.5 lbs · 6 reps` beside it and five RPE Chips (`6` to `8`), a tick under the prescribed RPE. The chevron goes back a face. None of these writes.
- `lock-screen-press` logs at the tapped Reps and RPE, restarts the rest, and shows the next Set's rest face. The Dynamic Island alone reads back `Logged 252.5×6 @7` for 3.5 s, and keeps it through a turn of the card. The log shows on the card itself, from one update: the fill runs back to full from the leading edge in a lighter green that settles to the plain fill by about 0.42 s, and the numbers roll to the next Set's, a Drop Set's to the weight the app worked out. On the Block's last Set, which no rest follows, the card shows that Set's Set Log on a whole fill with nothing to press for those 3.5 s, then the activity ends. Under Reduce Motion each face, the fill and the numbers fade in place.
- `lock-screen-refusal` writes nothing for a press the Set no longer matches, and reads back over the card. A Set that settled leads with `That tap changed nothing` over what it holds. Any other reason reads `Not logged` over why: `Too soon after the Reps. Tap the RPE again` for an RPE tap within a second of the card turning, after which the card is on its RPE face again, and `The mic is listening. Say the Set` or `The app’s mic is listening. Open to log` while a mic listens. No readback names the Block, Week, or Day.
- `lock-screen-needs-app` draws an arrow that opens the app where Log would be, for a Set whose card holds no weight (a `Drop` load, a Timed Set).
- `lock-screen-start` draws a timer glyph in the mic's place for an Exercise that opens on a hold. It opens the app, which counts down `Starts in 2`, then `Hold, N seconds left`.
- `lock-screen-rest-fill` drains a fill across the whole card over the rest, from the right, while the app is not running.
- `lock-screen-appearance` draws the card Day under Light, Night under Night, and under System in the phone's appearance as the app sent the update. The island stays Night under all three. Under System a change of the phone's appearance reaches the card at its next update. Choosing a look in Settings sends the card again at once.
- `lock-screen-island` offers the name, weight, target, guidance, and the Reps Chips then the RPE Chips on the expanded Dynamic Island with no Log tap, and gives way as type grows. A target too long for the line shrinks, then cuts off with `…`, so the name, the weight, the mic, the queue, and every chip stay whole.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Log a Set on the session stage and lock the phone. The card is on the Lock Screen for as long as the rest's activity runs.
- Tap Log, a Reps Chip, then an RPE Chip. The card shows the next Set.
- Tap the note button to read the Coach Note, and the close button to bring the numbers back.
- Long press the Dynamic Island with the app behind for the Reps Chips with no Log tap.

## Driving it with verify.sh

Preconditions:

- Every launch here takes `VERIFY_LIVE_ACTIVITIES=1`. `-UITEST_OFFLINE` keeps each write queued, so `Sync status` counts them.
- Run this on a simulator of your own, because locking it wedged a shared one's screenshots (`live-activity.md`, Gotchas). `verify.sh axe touch` lands where `tap` reports success and does nothing on a loaded host.
- A touch longer than about 0.2 s on `log-active-set-button` can start its hold, so give it `--delay 0.12` to `0.15`. Under load `tap --id` can report success and log nothing, so check `Sync status` after it.
- The tree holds only the card's frame, so read every control off a frame. The coordinates below are for the `large` type size (measured 2026-11-10).

## Gotchas

- How the system draws the card, and what a simulator shot of it can and cannot show, is in [the Lock Screen card's rendering](./lock-screen-rendering.md). Read it before judging motion, the fill, a countdown, or a colour off the simulator.
- Wait for a touch's intent, not for a delay. `xcrun simctl spawn "$SIM" log stream --style compact --process WorkoutTracker | grep 'perform()'` prints one `Invoking` line for each touch that reached the app. A touch that prints none landed off every button, which opens the app. A Log touch sent before the turn before it drew opened the app once (measured 2026-11-10).
- The same stream with `--process chronod | grep 'Received payload'` prints each update's size: about 760 bytes for a face, and about 900 while the island reads a Set Log back.
- `simctl io` cannot write under `~/Projects`, which fails with `Operation not permitted` for a screenshot and `OSStatus error -12204` for a take. Set `VERIFY_EVIDENCE` to a directory under `/tmp` for the drive, then move what you keep.
- Until `Allow` is answered the card is taller and `Don't Allow` sits near the chips' row, so a blind touch there refuses Live Activities for that install. Read `Allow` off the tree first. The prompt can arrive a few seconds after the first wake.
- Answering `Allow` after `simctl terminate` launches the app again in the background, so a take that has to show the card with no app running answers it before the terminate.
- `verify.sh launch` installs the app again, which brings the `Allow` prompt back. To bring a running app forward from the Lock Screen and keep the answer, run `xcrun simctl launch "$SIM" com.sunnypatel.WorkoutTracker`.
- Over Notification Center the tree also holds a `ListCell @14,0 374x219` that is not the card. Take the cell whose `y` is near the bottom, since the card's own can take seconds to list.
- The stage learns a weight changed on the app's card only through that card's mic (`mic?.shows` in `ActiveSetCard`), and the Simulator gives the card a mic only under `-UITEST_DICTATION "<transcript>"`. Without it the Lock Screen draws and logs the prefilled weight however the app's card was changed. With it, as on a phone, leaving the app redraws the card, so a change then a lock shows and logs the changed weight: `262.5 lbs` over the prefilled 252.5, then `Logged 262.5×6 @7` on the island (measured 2026-11-08). `LockScreenPress` refuses a press only while the Lock Screen still shows a weight the app's card has since changed.
- The app launched in the background by a press after `simctl terminate` loses its `-UITEST_*` flags and reads `No Session`, so a press against a killed fixture run proves nothing.
